Holding Your Own Hand (HYOH) is the passion project of Daisy Robin Caladia (Robin, in citations), with a goal to make music that is as unfiltered and unsanitized as possible. We haven't quite nailed down what genre HYOH is yet, but it's definitely akin to slacker rock, with several inspirations being slacker rock themselves.

Our debut album,
Watercolour Markers
is out now!
